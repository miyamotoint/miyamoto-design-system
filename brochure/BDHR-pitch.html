Section 01 — Capability

An American engineering and disaster response company.

We are an American global engineering and disaster response company with award-winning U.S. technical capability and a proven record of delivering for the United States at home and abroad.

We assess, secure, and restore essential systems — transportation networks, food and water supply chains, emergency access routes, housing, healthcare facilities, and first-responder infrastructure — ensuring speed, efficiency, and scale.

Our approach delivers immediate, life-saving impact while leveraging American innovation and private-sector strength.

We do not rely on long subcontracting chains. We deliver through direct U.S. capability, supported by local operational knowledge. We establish U.S.-owned companies at the local level to ensure effective, fast, and durable impact.
