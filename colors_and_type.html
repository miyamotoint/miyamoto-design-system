03 — In composition

The rules, in use.

Hairlines carry the grid. Accent appears once per composition. Paper, not white, is the page.

Case study · 014

A civic commission on the waterfront

The brief called for a building that would disappear into the shoreline at dusk and return, sharply, by morning. We answered with a façade of weathered steel and a plan that opens, rather than announces.

Completed in four phases across eighteen months, the project became the studio’s first use of the revised foundations.
